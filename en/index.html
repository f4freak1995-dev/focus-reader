The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy

"OK computer, I want full manual control now."

"You got it," said the computer. Several large desk panels slid open and banks of control consoles sprang up out of them, showering the crew with bits of expanded polystyrene packaging and balls of rolled-up cellophane: these controls had never been used before.

Zaphod stared at them wildly.

"OK, Ford," he said, "full retro thrust and ten degrees starboard. Or something..."

"Good luck guys," chirped the computer, "impact minus thirty seconds..." Ford leapt to the controls - only a few of them made any immediate sense to him so he pulled those.

The ship shook and screamed as its guidance rocked jets tried to push it every which way simultaneously. He released half of them and the ship span round in a tight arc and headed back the way it had come, straight towards the oncoming missiles.

Air cushions ballooned out of the walls in an instant as everyone was thrown against them. For a few seconds the inertial forces held them flattened and squirming for breath, unable to move.

Zaphod struggled and pushed in manic desperation and finally managed a savage kick at a small lever that formed part of the guidance system. The lever snapped off. The ship twisted sharply and rocketed upwards.

The crew were hurled violently back across the cabin. Ford's copy of The Hitch Hiker's Guide to the Galaxy smashed into another section of the control console with the combined result that the guide started to explain to anyone who cared to listen about the best ways of smuggling Antarean parakeet glands out of Antares (an Antarean parakeet gland stuck on a small stick is a revolting but much sought after cocktail delicacy and very large sums of money are often paid for them by very rich idiots who want to impress other very rich idiots), and the ship suddenly dropped out of the sky like a stone.

Reading modes

One book,
four ways to read.

Switch reading modes and keep your place in the original text.
Switch modes above and find the pace that feels right.

Pages

Read at ease

Two pages in wide windows, one in narrow windows. Contents, search, bookmarks and notes help you find your way through a book.

Sentence colors

See sentence boundaries

Soft alternating colors mark sentence boundaries while preserving context and the original text order.

ARSVP

Follow the words

Chinese segmentation, English word forms, name repair and candidate display durations, with personal difficult-word records. Timings remain adjustable engineering parameters.

Focus

Focus on the current word

Emphasize each word in its original sentence while keeping the context. Pause or switch modes and continue from your place.

Your local library

A place for your books.
And a little space for you.

On first launch, the app creates a local library and a book inbox. Put files there; the app discovers and imports them at startup or when you return to the library.

Identical content is not imported twice. Existing progress and notes are preserved. Original files and reading records are stored separately for easier management and backup.

Saved locally. Cross-device sync is not available yet.

Install or update on Windows+
  1. 1

    Download the installer

    Click the Windows download button, then find the .exe file in Downloads.

  2. 2

    Double-click and install

    The app and dictionaries install together. Update an existing installation without deleting your library.

  3. 3

    Open your book

    Open Focus Reader from the desktop or Start menu. Import a book or try the included sample.

A local library is created on first launch. You can also choose its folder in the app.

If the system shows an unknown publisher, confirm that the installer came from this page and check its checksum. Keep system security protections enabled.

Install and first open on Mac+

After downloading, install like this

  1. 1

    Open the downloaded file

    In Finder's Downloads folder, double-click the downloaded .dmg file.

  2. 2

    Drag it into Applications

    In the window that opens, drag Focus Reader into Applications folder.

  3. 3

    Open it and start reading

    Open Focus Reader from Finder's Applications folder. Your local library is created on first launch.

To add books later, put them in Documents → Focus Reader → Books; they are imported at startup or when you return to the library.

Does macOS say Apple cannot check the app for malicious software?
EXAMPLE MESSAGE

Focus Reader cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software.

The current Mac testing build has not been notarized by Apple, so macOS may show this message. This does not mean Apple has confirmed that the app is safe. The steps below apply only to messages about being unable to check the app or verify its developer. Confirm the installer came from this page, and continue only if you trust the app and have checked that the file has not been altered.

Follow these steps in order

  1. Dismiss the message first. Click OK or Done. Show in Finder only locates the file; it does not open the app.
  2. Install the app in Applications. If you are still in the DMG window with two icons, drag Focus Reader into Applications and wait for copying to finish.
  3. Try opening it from Applications once. Open Finder → Applications and double-click Focus Reader. If the same message appears, dismiss it and continue.
  4. Open Privacy & Security. Go to  → System Settings → Privacy & Security, then scroll down to the Security section.
  5. Find the blocked-app message for Focus Reader. Check that the message refers to Focus Reader, then click Open Anyway. Wording may vary with your macOS version.
  6. Confirm by clicking Open. Complete the macOS confirmation and click Open. Afterward, you can usually launch the app directly from Applications.

Can't find Open Anyway?

Check that copying in step 2 finished and that step 3 opened Focus Reader from Applications. Try opening it again, return to Privacy & Security and scroll down. A work- or school-managed Mac may restrict this action; contact its administrator.

Common questions

Before you begin.

Will my books be uploaded?

The current app uses a local library, with no account or cloud sync. This website does not read your books or include tracking scripts. External hosting services may record visits under their own policies.

Can it fully display scanned PDFs and every EPUB?

OCR is not available. Scanned PDFs can be viewed as original pages. Complex layouts, footnotes and publisher styling are still being improved. Encrypted EPUB content also has limitations.

Is the app open source?

The original reader source is currently private. The public website provides product information, release notes and license notices. Third-party components remain under their own licenses.

Are reading speed or comprehension gains guaranteed?

No. ARSVP and focus reading offer alternative presentations. Candidate timings need calibration through actual reading. The project does not claim validated improvements in comprehension.
